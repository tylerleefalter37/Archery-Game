📖 The Treestand Ballistics & Antiquities Codex

Downward pendulum trigonometry, vital anatomy, cut-on-contact points, and lithic dating.

I. Elevated Treestand Ballistics: True Horizontal Range

Perched 20 feet up in a mature white oak, gravity only acts on the horizontal distance, not the hypotenuse (line-of-sight).

  • Line-of-Sight vs. True Distance: A buck at 24 line-of-sight yards from a 20ft perch is actually only 21.8 horizontal yards away. Aiming for 24 yards results in shooting over the back.
  • Downward Entry / Low Exit: An arrow entering high on the ribcage and exiting low behind the offside front leg collapses both lungs and leaves an immediate, heavy blood trail.
  • Shoulder Scapula Plate: Traditional arrows hitting heavy bone fail to penetrate, snapping the wooden shaft and leaving the game unharmed.

II. Authentic Prehistoric Artifact Horizons

The Midwest and eastern hardwood forests host deep cultural stratigraphy washed clean in gravel bars:

  • Paleo-Indian (13,000–10,000 BP): Fluted Clovis and Folsom points knapped from Flint Ridge or translucent chalcedony. Characterized by long central flute channels for split-shaft hafting.
  • Archaic Period (10,000–3,000 BP): Ground slate bannerstones (atlatl weights), Dalton serrated knives, and hematite plummets.
  • Woodland & Mississippian (3,000–500 BP): Carved Catlinite raptor effigy pipes, Great Lakes hammered copper gorgets, and engraved shell masks.
